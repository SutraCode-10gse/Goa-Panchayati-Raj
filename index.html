Goa Panchayati Raj Structure

Under the Goa Panchayat Raj Act, 1994, rural local governance is structured as a two-tier system following the 73rd Constitutional Amendment. Gram Sabhas form the core deliberative assembly at the ward level.

District Level

Zilla Panchayats

North Goa & South Goa ZP (Sec 115-A)

Village Level

Village Panchayats

Sarpanch, Dy. Sarpanch & Secretary (Sec 7)

Electoral Level

Gram Sabhas

All Registered Voters in the Wards (Sec 3)

Recent Legislative Updates

2026 Bill

MLA Attendance & Secretary Autonomy

A bill introduced to allow local MLAs to attend Gram Sabhas as non-voting associate members (Sec 4-A) and clarify the Panchayat Secretary's execution powers (Sec 47).

2025 Act

Deemed Building Permission

Halved the approval decision period from 30 days to 15 days under Section 66, introducing automatic deemed clearance if the Panchayat is silent.

2023 Act

Sealing Unlicensed Businesses

Added Section 72-A, authorizing the Secretary to seal commercial setups operating without valid licenses, with safeguards for pending applications.
